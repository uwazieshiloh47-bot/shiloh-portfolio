Behind the Portfolio

About Me

Hello! My name is Shiloh Uwazie, a final-year Computer Science student at Louisiana State University at Alexandria specializing in cloud engineering. I focus on deploying and automating infrastructure on AWS, and my long-term goal is to become a Cloud Solutions Architect.

Why Cloud Engineering?

I have always been drawn to technology, especially cloud computing. The entire concept of the cloud has always been mind-blowing to me, and in some ways, it still is!

I am particularly interested in Cloud Engineering because I believe the role fits the way I think and solve problems. I am also open to exploring related opportunities throughout DevOps as I continue learning.

What I'm Working Toward

I am building a serverless visitor analytics API on AWS — Lambda, DynamoDB and API Gateway, with the whole stack defined in Terraform — while studying for the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification and deepening my work in Go, Python, and Linux.

Beyond Technology

Outside technology, I enjoy gaming, playing soccer on weekends, reading, and painting. I also consider myself a pretty great artist, haha!
