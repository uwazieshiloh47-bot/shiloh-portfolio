About Me

Hello! I am Shiloh Uwazie, a Computer Science Senior at Louisiana State University at Alexandria specializing in cloud engineering. I focus on deploying and automating infrastructure on AWS, and my long-term goal is to become a Cloud Solutions Architect.

I have always been drawn to technology, especially cloud computing. The entire concept of the cloud has always been mind-blowing to me, and in some ways, it still is! I am drawn to cloud engineering because the role fits the way I think and solve problems, and I am open to DevOps work as I keep learning.

Right now I am studying for the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification and deepening my work in Go, Python and Linux. The serverless visitor analytics API behind the count at the bottom of this page came out of that — Lambda, DynamoDB and API Gateway, with the whole stack defined in Terraform.

Outside technology, I enjoy gaming, playing soccer on weekends, reading, and painting. I also consider myself a pretty great artist, haha!
